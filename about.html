About

Sagtikos District supports units across the Towns of Babylon and Islip through district-wide programs, training, and coordinated resources.

This website is maintained by volunteers in support of the Sagtikos District program and is intended as a helpful resource for Scouts, leaders, and families.

This site is not an official website of Scouting America, the Suffolk County Council, Scouting Long Island, or any affiliated council or organization. It is not owned, operated, or formally endorsed by Scouting America or its councils.

Official information, policies, and announcements are provided through Scouting America and Suffolk County Council’s official channels.
